## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** Celsius Holdings reported Q1 2025 revenue of $329.3 million, a 7% year-over-year decline, framed by management as a temporary, well-understood softness rather than a structural issue; management said business fundamentals strengthened through the quarter and emphasized positive momentum heading into Q2. The revenue shortfall was attributed to three primary factors — slowed Q1 velocity, the timing and structure of the US distributor incentive program, and increased retail promotional programs — compounded by a tough comparison against a very strong Q1 2024 that included the nationwide launch of CELSIUS ESSENTIALS and elevated dedicated retail promotion takeovers. Positive drivers cited included gross margin expansion of 110 basis points to 52.3% (sourcing efficiencies), international revenue growth of 41% to $22.8 million, the closing of the Alani Nu acquisition on April 1 (a second $1 billion brand), Alani Nu retail sales up 88% surpassing $1 billion in trailing-52-week sales, and continued retail shelf-space gains. Acknowledged headwinds were the revenue decline itself, adjusted EBITDA margin compression to 21.2% (from 24.7%) driven by organizational investments and Alani Nu transaction expenses, and a cautious read on the consumer trading down from singles to multipacks. The strategic outlook was constructive and execution-focused: management reiterated confidence in leading the "modern energy" category, pointed to easier comps from June onward, new innovation (Playa Vibe, Retro Vibe, Mango Lemonade), a new summer marketing campaign, and previewed a dedicated public modeling call later in the quarter to address Alani Nu pro forma and purchase-accounting impacts. Overall tone was measured but confident — acknowledging a slow start while stressing improving exit velocity and a portfolio that now positions the company well for the peak summer selling season.

- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:**
  - **Bridging the North America revenue decline (Q2, Q4):** Analysts repeatedly tried to reconcile the ~2% retail sales growth with the high-single-digit reported North America decline, pressing for a clean read on underlying shipment versus depletion trends after stripping out promos, incentives, and timing noise.
  - **Allowances and destocking phasing (Q4):** Andrea Teixeira pushed hard for quantification and sequential phasing of distributor incentives/allowances, given they are not separately disclosed, seeking an apples-to-apples underlying growth figure.
  - **Gross margin sustainability and aluminum/tariff risk (Q5, Q8):** Analysts wanted to understand the drivers of the 52.3% gross margin, whether it is sustainable, and what watchouts exist from aluminum costs and tariffs in the back half.
  - **Alani Nu integration, cannibalization and cross-merchandising (Q6, Q7):** Analysts probed where Alani Nu out-performs core Celsius, the degree of cannibalization between the two brands, and the opportunity for co-branded coolers.
  - **Conviction in a core-brand inflection (Q7):** Gerald Pascarelli asked management to characterize its level of confidence that core Celsius revenue trends improve from here given innovation, shelf wins, easier comps and a new campaign.

- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:**
  - **Reported decline vs. scanner data credibility:** A central tension was the gap between the ~minus 4% scanner trend and the minus 7% reported revenue; management attributed the ~3-point gap to promos/incentives and non-DSD timing/pipe-fill, but the multi-component bridge left the "clean" underlying number dependent on management's own construction (Q2, Q4).
  - **Lack of allowance disclosure:** Andrea Teixeira explicitly noted allowances are "not disclosed" and "hard to calculate over a year," and management would only characterize the impact as "a couple of percentage points" per quarter without a full-year figure (Q4).
  - **Back-half gross margin uncertainty:** Management was firm on a ~50% core gross margin for the year but repeatedly hedged Q3/Q4 ("a little harder to draw a line in the sand"), citing unknown tariff and inflation outcomes (Q5, Q8).
  - **Costco decline vs. scanner strength:** Teixeira flagged a "steep decline at Costco"; management countered that Costco scanner data was up and attributed the reported softness to MVM pipe-fill timing and a working-capital nuance, not lost distribution (Q4).

- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **Alani Nu modeling call (later in Q2 2025, "late in the month or early June"):** Management committed to a public call providing pro forma combined-business reviews and purchase-accounting detail (inventory step-up, intangible/fixed-asset valuations, D&A). This is a catalyst because it will set the framework for how the Street models the consolidated Q2 and full-year P&L.
  - **Summer marketing campaign launch (Summer 2025):** Management said a "new exciting marketing campaign" and "bold story-driven campaigns" around the LIVE FIT identity begin this summer — a test of whether marketing investment can re-accelerate core Celsius velocity.
  - **Easier comps beginning June 2025:** Management said that after the next "four to five weeks" of peak revenue hurdles, Celsius begins cycling "much easier comps" from June — a near-term inflection point for the reported growth rate.
  - **Q2 2025 consolidated results (first quarter including Alani Nu):** Q2 will be the first period reflecting Alani Nu consolidation, the $900M debt draw, and inventory step-up noise — a key data point for the combined-company narrative.

- **Year-over-Year Comparability Factors:**
  - **Tough Comparisons:** Management repeatedly flagged that Q1 2025 lapped a very strong Q1 2024 that included the nationwide launch of CELSIUS ESSENTIALS, the largest distribution gains and innovation launches in company history, "elevated dedicated retail promotion takeovers," and the strongest shelf reset cycle in company history (reported on the Q1 2024 call). John Fieldly: "we were lapping a very strong first quarter in 2024 when we began nationwide distribution of CELSIUS ESSENTIALS and had elevated dedicated retail promotion takeovers providing strong tailwinds."
  - **Easy Comparisons:** Management said Celsius begins cycling "much easier comps" once it gets into June 2025, and that the back half of 2024 was "a little bit softer," setting up favorable second-half comparisons.
  - **Timing Shifts:** The CFO described "a little bit of pipe filling in the back half of the year [2024] versus having the programs run in Q1," meaning some non-DSD volume that historically ran in Q1 was instead loaded in Q4 2024 ("a little bit of a load in Q4"). He also cited "some noise at the very end of the quarter" and non-DSD club timing/sequencing from Q4 into Q1. A Costco MVM event in Q1 2025 created a "little bit of a pipe fill the quarter before or the month before."
  - **Distribution & Channel Dynamics:** Management noted continued retail shelf-space gains; average items selling per store in the Celsius brand family increased by 4.1 items in MULO+ with convenience. Management guided to expecting "15% to 20% shelf reset wins" (referenced by analyst Gerald Pascarelli). New foodservice distribution went nationwide and now represents ~13.4% of North America sales through PepsiCo. Circana data put crossover/cannibalization between Celsius and Alani Nu at about 15%.

- **Structural & Reporting Comparability Factors:**
  - **Currency & Pricing Impacts:** Management noted it took pricing in Q4 2024, which "has been rolling through," and discussed category pricing dynamics (Monster having priced ~75% of its portfolio). Celsius said it has flexibility for "opportunistic" additional pricing but is being cautious given consumer behavior.
  - **Portfolio & Mix Changes:** The Alani Nu acquisition closed April 1, 2025; it will be reflected in Q2 financial statements. The deal was funded with $900 million in debt, approximately $400 million in cash, and the remainder in stock. Management flagged inventory step-up purchase accounting will create Q2 noise as acquired inventory on hand at April 1 is sold through, plus increased D&A from intangible and fixed-asset valuations. Multipack offerings now represent ~28% (MULO+ Convenience) and ~55% (MULO+) of retail sales mix. The Big Beverage manufacturing facility is now fully integrated.
  - **Accounting & Calendar Effects:** The CFO noted a Costco-specific nuance — revenue as a percentage of sales / ARR as a percentage of sales — described as "just timing of payments, more of a working capital component." Q1 2025 cash of $977 million reflects the position prior to the Alani Nu close.
  - **Macro & Seasonal Anomalies:** Management characterized the broader operating environment as "dynamic" with "uncertain economic times," "a challenging consumer environment, increased competition and strong pricing action by other category players." The CFO repeatedly flagged unknown tariff outcomes as a back-half risk.

- **Top Priority Follow-Up Questions:**

#### Question 1 — Underlying North America Depletion Trend

**Building Blocks:** Reference the specific scanner-vs-reported bridge management gave (Context Reinstatement) + bracket the residual gap (Bracketing) + force a single clean number (Precision).
**Q (Good):** "You bridged the minus 4% scanner trend to minus 7% reported with a couple of points of promos and incentives — of the remaining gap, is the non-DSD pipe-fill timing closer to 1 point or 3 points, and what does that imply for the clean underlying North America depletion rate exiting March?"

- **Rationale:** Management gave a multi-component bridge but never landed on a single clean underlying depletion figure, which is essential for modeling the Q2 reacceleration.
- **Question Type:** Context Reinstatement + Bracketing
- **Question Topic(s):** Revenue bridge, depletions, shipment timing
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
- **Question Importance Rank:** 1

#### Question 2 — Full-Year Allowance Impact

**Building Blocks:** Acknowledge non-disclosure (Empathetic Prologue) + establish a baseline reference (Baseline Establishment) + request a net full-year figure (Component Breakdown).
**Q (Good):** "Recognizing allowances aren't separately disclosed, what is the net full-year 2025 revenue impact from the distributor incentive and promotional allowance changes, given you've described roughly two points of pressure in each of Q1 and Q2 and a roughly two-point benefit in each of Q3 and Q4?"

- **Rationale:** Management quantified quarterly direction but never confirmed whether the full-year impact nets to roughly zero or remains a net drag.
- **Question Type:** Empathetic Prologue + Baseline Establishment
- **Question Topic(s):** Allowances, distributor incentives, full-year revenue
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
- **Question Importance Rank:** 2

#### Question 3 — Core Gross Margin Back-Half Path

**Building Blocks:** Use the management-stated ~50% anchor (Time-Bound Context) + request driver decomposition (Component Breakdown).
**Q (Good):** "Of the variance that could move core Celsius gross margin off the roughly 50% full-year level, how much is aluminum input cost versus tariff exposure, and at what aluminum price level does the 50% framework no longer hold?"

- **Rationale:** Management gave a ~50% core margin target but characterized Q3/Q4 only as "an unknown" without quantifying sensitivities.
- **Question Type:** Scenario Testing + Component Breakdown
- **Question Topic(s):** Gross margin, aluminum, tariffs
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 2
- **Question Importance Rank:** 3

#### Question 4 — Alani Nu Standalone Margin Ramp

**Building Blocks:** Reinstate the CFO's "mid-40s" remark (Context Reinstatement) + bound the timeline precisely (Time-Bounding).
**Q (Good):** "You said Alani Nu is roughly 18 to 24 months behind Celsius on the P&L profile and would sit in the low-end of the mid-40s on gross margin excluding the step-up — in which specific quarter do you expect Alani Nu gross margin to reach Celsius's level?"

- **Rationale:** Management gave a directional "mid-40s, 18-24 months behind" framing but no specific convergence quarter, leaving the consolidated margin trajectory ambiguous.
- **Question Type:** Context Reinstatement + Time-Bounding
- **Question Topic(s):** Alani Nu margins, integration, consolidated P&L

**Q (Good):** "Where do you expect core Celsius retail velocity to be exiting Q2 versus the improved exit rate you saw in late Q1, and what specifically drives that delta?"

- **Rationale:** Management repeatedly cited "improving velocity" but never quantified the exit rate or the expected Q2 trajectory.
- **Question Type:** Reverse Chronology + Baseline Establishment
