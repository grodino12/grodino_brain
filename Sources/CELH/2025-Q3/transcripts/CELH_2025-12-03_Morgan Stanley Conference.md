## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** This was a fireside-chat appearance, not an earnings call, so there were no headline period results to beat or miss; instead management used the forum to reframe the narrative after a sharp post-Q3 stock decline. CFO Jarrod Langhans characterized Q3 2025 as "a phenomenal quarter" on the top line, gross margin and EBITDA margin, and explained that the apparent net-sales "disconnect" versus buy-side expectations was driven primarily by promotional activity on brand Celsius that brought cases back at a slightly lower net sales per case, plus timing of Amazon Prime load-ins. The primary positive drivers cited were the turnaround of brand Celsius (from low-single-digit negative scanner growth early in the year to double-digit growth and category-exceeding performance recently, ~20% in convenience), strong Alani Nu growth, and the successful migration of "high 80s" percent of the Alani DSD system into the Pepsi network as of December 1. Management acknowledged headwinds: Q4 2025 will see "the most pressure" on gross margin from integration one-time charges, returns/scrap from the Pepsi distribution transition, tariff activity, lower-margin Rockstar mix, and rising aluminum/Midwest premium costs; management also flagged potential year-end inventory management by Pepsi as a possible timing item. The strategic outlook was confidently optimistic: management framed a sequential margin recovery path (Q4 pressured, then improving each quarter, back to "low 50s" gross margin in the back half of 2026, expanding into Q4 2026 and 2027), positioned the now three-brand portfolio (Celsius, Alani, Rockstar) plus the Pepsi captaincy as a structural advantage, and described the company as "set up for a fantastic 2026."
- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:** Analyst Eric Serotta concentrated on the following themes —
  - The mechanics of the Q3 2025 net-sales "disconnect" and what the market misunderstood (Question 1).
  - Q4 2025 top-line and margin visibility given the Alani-into-Pepsi move, Rockstar integration, and potential Pepsi year-end inventory management (Questions 2–4).
  - The 2026 gross margin and OpEx bridge — confidence in reaching the low-50s then mid-50s gross margin (Question 7).
  - The sustainability of the brand Celsius turnaround and the long-term runway/LTO "treadmill" risk for Alani (Questions 8, 12).
  - Pricing/RGM opportunity, capital allocation (buyback, debt paydown), and the competitive moat in sugar-free energy (Questions 6, 14, 13).
- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:**
  - The credibility of the Q3 net-sales miss explanation — Serotta opened by noting the stock was "down sharply" on "very high and lofty expectations" and pressed on what the market misunderstood (Question 1).
  - Q4 2025 visibility and the risk of a Pepsi year-end inventory drawdown — Serotta explicitly invoked the "$100 million plus in third quarter '24" precedent and pressed whether management had concrete visibility or was simply "leaving some cushion" (Questions 3–4); Langhans downplayed it as immaterial timing.
  - Whether the Alani LTO model is a sustainable "hamster wheel" / "treadmill" of cycling prior-year limited-time offers (Question 12).
  - Confidence in the gross margin recovery path given simultaneous cost headwinds (aluminum, tariffs, Rockstar dilution) versus Alani synergies (Question 7).
  - Long-term category structure — whether Celsius can durably erode the Red Bull/Monster ~70% combined share duopoly (Question 15).
- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **Completion of the Alani DSD migration into Pepsi (by Q1 2026):** Management said the Alani integration "will be completed by Q1," with Tier 1/Tier 2 (Walmart, Target) resets across Q1–Q2; full visibility into the business expected by end of Q2 2026.
  - **Rockstar integration completion (first half 2026):** "Rockstar will be done in the first half of the year," with intentional SKU rationalization toward a ~$250M FY2026 run rate.
  - **Gross margin recovery to "low 50s" in back-half 2026:** A key proof point for the margin-bridge thesis after a pressured Q4 2025.
  - **Big Beverage second production line online in Q3 2026:** Expected to be "fully baked in" by Q4 2026, supporting continued margin expansion.
  - **FY2026 results / Alani LTO program execution:** First LTO run with Alani inside the Pepsi system is positioned as a key execution test.
- **Year-over-Year Comparability Factors:**
  - **Tough Comparisons:** Management noted Q3 2024 included "some optimization" (inventory reductions); comparing Q3 2023 to Q3 2025, volume is up "probably a 10% to 11%" — the depressed Q3 2024 base distorts straight YoY reads. Q3 2025 brand Celsius cases came back via promotional activity "at a slightly lower case rate, so net sales per case," compressing net-sales growth relative to volume growth.
  - **Easy Comparisons:** Brand Celsius was at "low single digits earlier in the year, negative" and is now "into the double digits"; the weak early-2025 base sets up easier comps as 2026 progresses.
  - **Timing Shifts:** Q3 2025 included "timing of some load-ins with Amazon Prime." Q4 2025 will carry timing/sequencing noise from the Rockstar integration (finished-goods revenue on the top line vs. a brand-transition-service piece in other income) and the Alani-into-Pepsi move; potential Pepsi year-end inventory management framed as a timing issue ("a day is $5 million or so").
  - **Distribution & Channel Dynamics:** "High 80s" percent of the Alani DSD system moved into Pepsi as of 12/1. Alani Tier 3/Tier 4 (Pepsi-controlled independent convenience, food service at colleges/universities) ramp in Q1 2026; Tier 1/Tier 2 (Walmart, Target) reset across Q1–Q2 2026. Rockstar SKU rationalization toward a ~$250M FY2026 run rate frees shelf space.
- **Structural & Reporting Comparability Factors:**
  - **Currency & Pricing Impacts:** No FX guidance given. On pricing, management sees "pricing as an opportunity" but had "nothing specific to call out," flagging RGM (revenue growth management) as "a big focus for us in '26 and beyond."
  - **Portfolio & Mix Changes:** The portfolio moved from single-brand (Celsius) a year ago to three brands (Celsius, Alani, Rockstar). Rockstar "is currently a lower margin business," diluting consolidated gross margin; Alani synergies are an offsetting tailwind expected fully baked from April 2026. Rockstar revenue is partly recognized as finished-goods top line and partly as a brand-transition service in other income.
  - **Accounting & Calendar Effects:** Rockstar finished-goods revenue flows to the top line while a brand-transition-service component flows through other income — a presentation nuance affecting reported revenue comparability during the integration.
  - **Macro & Seasonal Anomalies:** Management noted aluminum metal cost and the Midwest premium "have continued to rise"; benefit from prior-year forward purchases of aluminum is anniversarying. "Some tariff activity out there" is an additional cost factor for Q4 2025 and into 2026.

#### Core Questioning Principles

**Q1 — Q4 2025 Gross Margin Magnitude**

**Building Blocks:** Presumptive framing + Component Breakdown + Time-Bounding. **Q:** "Of the gross margin pressure you expect in Q4 2025, what share comes from one-time integration charges and Pepsi-transition returns and scrap versus the ongoing drag from Rockstar mix and aluminum?"

- **Rationale:** Management described Q4 2025 as carrying "the most pressure" and listed multiple drivers but did not size any of them, which is critical for modeling the trough quarter.
- **Question Type:** Presumptive Question with Component Breakdown
- **Question Topic(s):** Gross margin, integration costs, Q4 2025 modeling
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
- **Question Importance Rank:** 1

**Q2 — "Low 50s" Gross Margin Definition**

**Building Blocks:** Bracketing + Baseline Establishment. **Q:** "When you say back-half 2026 gross margin returns to the 'low 50s,' is that closer to 50% or 53%, and what is the comparable back-half 2025 starting point?"

- **Rationale:** "Low 50s" is a qualitative range; quantifying it and anchoring against a 2025 baseline is essential to the margin-recovery thesis.
- **Question Type:** Bracketing + Baseline Establishment
- **Question Topic(s):** Gross margin trajectory, 2026 outlook
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
- **Question Importance Rank:** 2

**Q3 — Net Sales Per Case Trajectory**

**Building Blocks:** Context Reinstatement + Time-Bounding. **Q:** "You said the Q3 brand Celsius cases came back at a lower net sales per case because of promotional activity — over the next two quarters, do you expect net sales per case to recover toward prior levels, or is this the new run rate?"

- **Rationale:** The lower net-sales-per-case effect was the "primary disconnect" in Q3; whether it normalizes determines the gap between volume and revenue growth going forward.
- **Question Type:** Context Reinstatement + Time-Bounding
- **Question Topic(s):** Promotional intensity, net revenue per case, pricing
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 2
- **Question Importance Rank:** 3

**Q4 — Rockstar FY2026 Run-Rate Composition**

**Building Blocks:** Component Breakdown + Baseline Establishment. **Q:** "Of the roughly $250 million Rockstar run rate you cited for 2026, how much is finished-goods revenue on the top line versus the brand-transition-service piece in other income?"

- **Rationale:** Management split Rockstar economics across two P&L lines; the mix materially affects reported top-line growth and other income modeling.
- **Question Type:** Component Breakdown
- **Question Topic(s):** Rockstar, revenue recognition, FY2026 modeling
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 2
- **Question Importance Rank:** 4

**Q5 — Pepsi Year-End Inventory Visibility**

**Building Blocks:** Presumptive framing + Bracketing. **Q:** "What direct communication have you received from Pepsi on year-end inventory plans, and is the potential timing impact closer to a couple of days or a full week of shipments?"

- **Rationale:** Management acknowledged a possible "week" shutdown scenario (~$5M/day) but said there was no specific visibility; pinning down the bracket reduces a key Q4 uncertainty.
- **Question Type:** Presumptive Question with Bracketing
- **Question Topic(s):** Channel inventory, Q4 2025 shipments, Pepsi relationship
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 3
- **Question Importance Rank:** 5
