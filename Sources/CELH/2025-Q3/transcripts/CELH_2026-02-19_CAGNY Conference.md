## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** This was a CAGNY conference investor presentation, so there were no headline period results to beat or miss; instead management used the platform to articulate the strategic transformation of Celsius Holdings from a single-brand company into a three-brand "total energy" / "functional beverage platform." CEO John Fieldly framed 2025 as a "defining year" in which the company validated its strategy after acquiring Alani Nu and Rockstar and strengthening the PepsiCo partnership. Positive drivers cited: an approximately 20% U.S. energy share (distribution up over 8% YoY), 99.5% ACV through the PepsiCo DSD network, $5.2 billion of FY2025 portfolio retail sales, 31% YoY consumption growth in Q3 2025, and structural category tailwinds — energy now 20% of LRB sales (up from ~14% five years ago) with 85% of energy growth coming from zero sugar. Headwinds and challenges were largely addressed indirectly: management acknowledged Rockstar requires a "disciplined and phased approach" focused first on "stabilizing performance," that international remains "a smaller portion of our business today," that the business has scaled with "increasing complexity," and that the operating model "must change" in how it executes. The strategic outlook was decidedly confident and forward-looking — management positioned the company around "more people, more places, more often," an ambition to move "from big brands to mega brands," a deepening PepsiCo "energy captain" partnership, and disciplined revenue growth management (RGM) to drive "higher quality growth, 2026 and beyond." The overall tone was optimistic, vision-led, and emphatic about durability and execution discipline.
