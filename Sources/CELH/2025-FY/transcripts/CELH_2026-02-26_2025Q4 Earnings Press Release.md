## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** Celsius Holdings reported record full-year 2025 revenue of $2,515.3 million, up 85.5% year-over-year, characterized by CEO John Fieldly as a "defining year." The headline narrative emphasizes scale and the build-out of a three-brand "Modern Energy" portfolio (CELSIUS, Alani Nu, Rockstar Energy), with the company reaching an approximate 20% dollar share of the U.S. energy drink category in Q4 2025 as PepsiCo's energy category captain. The release contains no explicit consensus beat/miss commentary. Positive drivers: full-year revenue growth was primarily driven by $1,001.9 million of Alani Nu revenue (Q2–Q4 2025); CELSIUS brand revenue grew 7.5% to $1,457.7 million; Q4 revenue grew 117% to $721.6 million; Q4 adjusted EBITDA was $134.1 million (+113%) and FY adjusted EBITDA was $619.6 million (+142%, 24.6% margin); Q4 adjusted diluted EPS was $0.26 (+86%) and FY adjusted diluted EPS was $1.34 (+91%); FY gross margin improved 20 bps to 50.4%; and international revenue grew 24% to $92.8 million. Headwinds and challenges: Q4 GAAP gross margin compressed 280 bps to 47.4% on Rockstar dilution, integration costs, and tariffs; CELSIUS brand revenue declined ~8% in Q4 on temporary integration-related timing dynamics; FY GAAP net income fell 26% to $108.0 million and FY GAAP diluted EPS declined 44% to $0.25 (versus $0.45), pressured by $327 million of distributor termination costs and $60 million of acquisition-related costs; Rockstar Energy retail sales declined 11% YoY. The strategic outlook is confident and execution-focused: management reaffirmed the Alani Nu integration completing by end of Q1 2026 and Rockstar by end of Q2 2026, expects gross margins to expand across 2026 and return to a "low 50s" profile, and entered 2026 "with positive momentum, scale and confidence."
- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:** Not applicable — this is a press release with no question-and-answer session. No analyst questions were captured. (See the companion same-day earnings call transcript file for analyst Q&A.)
- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:** Not applicable — no Q&A in this press release. The release itself preemptively addresses the most contentious item: the gap between CELSIUS brand's ~8% Q4 GAAP revenue decline and its +13% U.S. tracked retail sales, attributing it to "anticipated, temporary integration-related timing dynamics" and a "short term misalignment between shipments, distributor depletions, and promotional activity" that is "not reflective of underlying retail demand trends."
- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **Alani Nu integration completion (end of Q1 2026):** Reaffirmed; completes cost-structure alignment and should reduce integration timing noise.
  - **Rockstar Energy integration completion (end of Q2 2026):** Reaffirmed; full integration enabling improved margin structure.
  - **Gross margin recovery to "low 50s" across 2026:** A key proof point after the 47.4% Q4 trough.
  - **Alani Nu ACV ramp:** ACV increased from ~87% at the start of Q4 2025 to 94.2% as of the week ended Feb. 1, 2026 — continued ramp is a forward distribution catalyst.
  - **Continued early-2026 momentum:** The release notes "continued strong growth across January and February of 2026" for both CELSIUS and Alani Nu.
- **Year-over-Year Comparability Factors:**
  - **Tough Comparisons:** Q4 2025 GAAP gross margin of 47.4% was down 280 bps from Q4 2024's 50.2% (Rockstar dilution, integration costs, tariffs) — a structurally lower base. FY2024 GAAP net income of $145.1 million benefited from $39.3 million of net interest income and a lower expense base; FY2025 net income of $108.0 million carries $327 million of distributor termination costs and $49.0 million of interest expense, distorting straight YoY net income / EPS comparisons.
  - **Easy Comparisons:** Not explicitly flagged as easy comps in the release, though the prior-year Q4 2024 included $54.0 million of legal settlement costs (litigation + SEC settlement) and $9.4 million of penalties — non-recurring items that depressed the Q4 2024 base.
  - **Timing Shifts:** CELSIUS brand revenue declined ~8% in Q4 2025 due to anticipated, temporary integration-related timing dynamics and order sequencing from the largest distributor, creating a short-term misalignment between shipments, distributor depletions, and promotional activity. There was a net positive impact of ~$25 million between the Alani Nu load-in to PepsiCo and the CELSIUS brand timing differences in Q4 2025.
  - **Distribution & Channel Dynamics:** Celsius Holdings increased total distribution points by 15% (FY) and achieved 99.5% ACV; CELSIUS brand TDPs +20% (98.5% ACV); Alani Nu TDPs +39% (92.6% ACV); Rockstar Energy TDPs -17% (85.4% ACV). Alani Nu ACV rose from ~87% at the start of Q4 2025 to 94.2% by Feb. 1, 2026 following the Dec. 1, 2025 transition.
  - **Structural:** The release notes "fluctuations can occur between reported results and tracked data" with "more consistent alignment over longer time periods."
  - **Acquisition Effects:** Alani Nu was acquired April 1, 2025 (in revenue for Q2–Q4 2025 only); Rockstar Energy was acquired August 28, 2025 — making FY2025 a partial-year contribution for both, an inherent non-comparability with FY2026.
  - **Inventory step-up:** A non-cash $22.4 million inventory valuation step-up from the Alani Nu and Rockstar acquisitions hit FY2025 cost of revenue (a non-recurring item).
- **Structural & Reporting Comparability Factors:**
  - **Currency & Pricing Impacts:** FY2025 foreign currency translation was a $6.4 million gain (vs. a $2.5 million loss in FY2024). No pricing-action figures disclosed.
  - **Portfolio & Mix Changes:** The most material structural change is the portfolio composition — Alani Nu (acquired April 1, 2025; $1,001.9 million FY2025 revenue) and Rockstar Energy (acquired August 28, 2025; $55.6 million FY2025 revenue) are now consolidated, fundamentally changing revenue, margin, and expense comparability. Rockstar Energy retail sales declined 11% YoY with TDPs down 17%.
  - **Accounting & Calendar Effects:** FY2025 SG&A includes $327 million of distributor termination costs and $60 million of acquisition-related costs; the total distributor buyout obligation recorded by year-end 2025 was ~$327.5 million ($246.7 million in Q3 2025 + $80.8 million in Q4 2025). The balance sheet shows $141.1 million of restricted cash (vs. $0 in 2024), $264.1 million of accrued distributor termination fees, $25.0 million of contingent consideration, $669.9 million of long-term debt (vs. $0), and $907.6 million of newly issued Series B convertible preferred stock — all structural changes affecting period-over-period balance sheet comparability.
  - **Macro & Seasonal Anomalies:** The release cites tariffs and integration costs as drivers of Q4 gross margin compression. (The companion earnings call elaborates on the Midwest aluminum premium.)
