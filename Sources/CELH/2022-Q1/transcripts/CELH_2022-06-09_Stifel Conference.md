## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** In a fireside chat at the Stifel 2022 Cross Sector Insight Conference, CEO John Fieldly and VP of Business Development Toby David framed Celsius as a category leader executing a deliberate "backdooring" of the energy category — having built brand credibility and authenticity in fitness/health-and-wellness channels before pushing into traditional convenience. Management emphasized Celsius's incrementality: it accounted for 37% of the energy category's ~$100 million of recent growth (the largest single contributor, ahead of Red Bull and Monster), with roughly 90% of that contribution non-cannibalizing. They cited a ~4 category share (roughly doubled from ~2% over twelve months), the #2 Amazon position (~8.4 share), and the recent passing of Rockstar to become the #4 energy brand, with Bang (the #3 brand) the next stated target. The central growth thesis is the convenience channel — 2022 is "really our first year in convenience" with ~68,000 doors (roughly half of Nielsen/IRI-reporting stores), anchored by the new Circle K rollout (~5,000 doors, ~8 flavors nationwide). Management explained the unusual phenomenon of velocity rising even as distribution expands (DSD conversion delivers a 100%+ lift; new doors are the most productive). On pricing, Fieldly confirmed an April 1 frontline (list) price increase under a three-tier rollout fully in by Q4, targeting a $2.59–$2.79 convenience price point (premium to Monster, below Red Bull). On margins, management acknowledged it is deliberately over-investing ahead of revenue, benchmarking against Monster's ~$700–800 million inflection point. The tone was highly confident, growth-oriented, and execution-focused.
- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:**
  - Astrachan probed **incrementality and share sourcing** — whether Celsius is genuinely incremental rather than sourcing from Monster/Red Bull, and how that piece of the category coexists with legacy players (Questions 1, 2).
  - **Shelf-space and retailer dynamics** — whether Celsius wins incremental space or takes it from others, and how the incrementality argument lands with buyers (Questions 3, 4).
  - **Market share progression** — how to benchmark the path from a 4 share given high-double-digit shares in mature markets like Florida (Question 5).
  - The **velocity-versus-distribution** dynamic — why velocity rises as distribution expands, and the incremental-versus-repeat customer mix (Questions 6, 7, 8).
  - **Pricing and promotion** — the April 1 price increase mechanics and stickiness, price-point thresholds, the give-back of pre-list-price promotional pricing, and the right promotional cadence; plus the **long-term margin leverage** opportunity (Questions 9–13).
- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:**
  - Management **declined to disclose the headline price-increase magnitude**, confirming only the April 1 timing, the three-tier rollout fully in by Q4, and a target $2.59–$2.79 convenience price point (Questions 9, 10).
  - Toby David **gave no direct answer on share progression timing**, saying only that overtaking Bang (the #3 brand) is a "relatively near future" opportunity without committing to months or years (Question 5).
  - Astrachan pushed on whether **half-the-year promotional cadence is actually the right level** ("Doesn't mean that it's right"); Fieldly conceded it is an industry norm expected by retailers rather than a proven optimum (Question 12).
  - Astrachan explicitly **deflected the gross-margin/input-cost discussion** as "a waste of time," focusing instead on pricing — and the margin-leverage question was compressed into the final 30 seconds, limiting depth (Questions 9, 13).
- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **First full year in convenience (~68,000 doors):** 2022 is framed as a building year through summer beverage season into the NACS-led selling season in October — a key test of the convenience thesis.
  - **NACS October selling season / next-year resets:** Management is already positioning for next year's resets, where it aims to fight for a second shelf rather than one.
  - **Three-tier price increase fully in by Q4:** The April 1 frontline price increase completes in Q4 — a measurable pricing-power and elasticity test.
  - **Numerator data on the next earnings call:** Management acquired Numerator and will share new analytical data on the next earnings call.
  - **Untapped distribution (Walgreens, Dollar channel, Kroger beverage set):** Significant unopened distribution remains — Walgreens, Dollar Tree, and improved Kroger beverage-set placement.
  - **Passing Bang to become the #3 energy brand:** Management's next stated milestone, expected in the "relatively near future."

  - **Easy Comparisons:** The club channel scaled from a Q2-2021 Costco test to a sizable Q1 2022 business, lapping a near-zero prior-year base; market share roughly doubled from ~2% to ~4% over twelve months.
  - **Timing Shifts:** The three-tier price-increase rollout — with some customers on annual contracts — defers full pricing realization to Q4 and means it will not be "100% in our numbers" immediately.

#### Question 1 - Incrementality & Share Sourcing

- **Q: Mark Astrachan (Stifel)**: Asked whether Celsius's functional/performance positioning is largely incremental rather than sourcing from Monster/Red Bull, how much is incremental at a category level, and how the functional-energy space coexists with legacy players over time.
  - "...how much do you think is incremental from a category standpoint? And C, kind of where do you see that piece of the category... playing out over time? And does it ultimately coexist with those legacy entrants?"
