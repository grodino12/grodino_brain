## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** Celsius Holdings delivered a strong Q2 2025, with revenue of $739.3 million up 84% year-over-year — a result management framed as proof of the multi-brand portfolio strategy following the April 1, 2025 close of the Alani Nu acquisition. The primary positive driver was Alani Nu's breakout performance ($301.2 million of revenue, retail dollar sales up 129%), powered by record limited-time-offer flavors (Cotton Candy, Sherbert Swirl), while the core Celsius brand returned to growth (revenue +9%, $438.1 million). Adjusted EBITDA was a record at $210 million (up 109%) and adjusted diluted EPS reached $0.47 versus $0.28 a year ago. Acknowledged headwinds were a 50 bps year-over-year decline in consolidated gross margin to 51.5% (despite the underlying number being closer to 54%+ before a $21.7 million purchase-accounting inventory step-up), the structurally lower margin profile of Alani Nu, and elevated G&A from $16 million of acquisition costs plus a $13.8 million earn-out re-measurement to its $25 million maximum. The strategic outlook is constructive but cautious on margin: management expects tariffs and rising aluminum / Midwest premium / LME costs to pressure gross margin in Q3 and Q4, guiding to "low 50s%" gross margin "as things are today, if nothing changes." Overall tone was confident and execution-focused, emphasizing disciplined integration, the $50 million two-year synergy target, and an innovation-led second half.

- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:**
  - **Gross margin drivers and forward trajectory (Q1):** Analysts wanted the better-than-expected Q2 margin disaggregated and a framework for the back half given tariffs, aluminum, and the Midwest premium.
  - **Alani Nu shipments versus takeaway (Q2):** Analysts probed whether robust reported Alani Nu shipments were inflated by pre-close LTO sell-in and how to model Witch's Brew and the back half.
  - **International expansion plans (Q3):** Analysts sought specifics on second-half international market expansion.
  - **Costco / club-channel dynamics (Q4):** Analysts wanted the Costco revenue contribution and whether an MVM coupon program inflated the quarter.
  - **Celsius brand reacceleration and innovation (Q5):** Analysts wanted the drivers of Celsius's return to growth (marketing, comps, innovation) and the back-half innovation slate.
  - **Alani Nu back-half pacing and household penetration (Q6) and Q3 puts-and-takes (Q7):** Analysts wanted pacing into tougher comps, new-versus-existing household contribution, and clarity on promo timing and the prior-year distribution comp.

- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:**
  - **Margin sustainability versus reported strength:** Peter Grom noted Q2 margin beat the prior 47%–49% framework and was "even stronger" after the inventory adjustment; management repeatedly hedged ("I'll do my best to not have everyone kind of get over their skis"), guiding only to "low 50s%" conditional on no change in tariffs/aluminum (Q1).
  - **Shipments versus scanner takeaway for Alani Nu:** Eric Serotta pressed whether robust shipments reflected pre-close LTO sell-in; management conceded "it's not a perfect match" but pointed to ~129% scanner growth versus ~106% reported as evidence sell-through was tracking (Q2).
  - **Reported Celsius growth versus scanner growth:** The CFO disclosed reported Celsius brand growth of 9% versus only ~3% scanner growth, attributing the gap to a favorable prior-year distributor inventory comp — a quality-of-growth question left only partially quantified (Prepared Remarks).
  - **Forward visibility:** When Andrea Teixeira pressed on Q3 unfolding and the prior-year ~$20 million distribution easy comp, management declined: "we're not going to provide forward-looking information. It's just, it's really difficult right now" (Q7).

- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **Witch's Brew and Pumpkin Cream / Winter Wonderland LTOs (Q3–Q4 2025):** Management said it "really need[s] those to be as successful as they were last year"; Alani Nu's back-half growth hinges on lapping the record Cotton Candy / Sherbert Swirl LTOs.
  - **Celsius first-ever LTO (back half 2025):** The Celsius brand's first limited-time offer launches in the back half / winter season — a test of the brand's broadened innovation playbook.
  - **First national Celsius TV commercial during NFL broadcasts (fall 2025):** Management called it "a major milestone" for brand awareness.
  - **Tariff / aluminum / LME cost trajectory (Q3–Q4 2025):** Management flagged tariffs hitting raw-material costs in Q3 and Q4 as the key swing factor for whether margin holds in the low 50s.
  - **Live. Fit. Go. marketing campaign data (next few weeks post-call):** Management said full analytics on the new campaign were due "in the next few weeks," a near-term read on household-penetration acceleration.

- **Year-over-Year Comparability Factors:**
  - **Tough Comparisons:** Management flagged tougher Alani Nu comps in the back half. The CFO cited 2024 Alani Nu quarterly revenue progression of "136, 146, 166, 157" ($ millions), noting "the comps are a little tougher" and that Q2 2025 lapped record Cotton Candy and Sherbert Swirl LTOs ("our top two LTOs ever with Alani").
  - **Easy Comparisons:** Andrea Teixeira referenced "about $20 million in the distribution issues last year"; the CFO had noted reported Celsius results "benefited from a favorable comp from the prior year inventory movements at our largest distributor" — i.e., a depressed prior-year base lifted reported Celsius growth (~9% reported vs. ~3% scanner). Management declined to quantify the back-half tailwind from this.
  - **Timing Shifts:** Management moved the Witch's Brew launch "forward several weeks," creating weekly-tracking puts and takes. A Costco promotional activity shifted into Q3. Management also "optimize[d] some of our promotions moving from Q1 into the summer season."
  - **Distribution & Channel Dynamics:** Club channel (Costco, Sam's, BJ's) drove a portfolio uplift of "about 17% for the quarter," partly from an MVM/MDM coupon program at Costco. Alani Nu operates "mainly an independent network." Celsius food service volume grew 9.8% year-over-year, ~12% of North America Celsius brand sales to PepsiCo.
  - **Top Priority Follow-Up Questions:** See STEP 4 follow-up question block below.

- **Structural & Reporting Comparability Factors:**
  - **Currency & Pricing Impacts:** No FX guidance quantified. On pricing, the CFO noted "price hikes we had for a good part of the year that helped with the aluminum" — historical Celsius pricing actions cushioned aluminum cost inflation in Q2.
  - **Portfolio & Mix Changes:** Alani Nu acquired April 1, 2025; Q2 2025 is the first quarter Alani Nu is in the consolidated results, contributing $301.2 million of revenue and structurally lowering consolidated gross margin. Management guided to $50 million of run-rate cost synergies over two years post-closing.
  - **Accounting & Calendar Effects:** Q2 gross margin absorbed a $21.7 million purchase-accounting inventory step-up; excluding it, management said margin was "more of a 54% or 54%+ margin." G&A included $16 million of acquisition-related costs and a $13.8 million contingent-consideration (earn-out) charge re-measured to the $25 million maximum payout. Inventory recorded on FIFO meant "we did not see a material impact from tariffs in the second quarter."
  - **Macro & Seasonal Anomalies:** Management cited double-digit energy-category growth in 2025 and ">15% year-over-year retail sales growth" for the energy category, a favorable demand backdrop rather than an anomaly.

#### Top Priority Follow-Up Questions

**Topic: Gross Margin Cost Sensitivity**

**Q (Good):** "Of the move from your prior 47%–49% framework to a 'low 50s%' expectation, how many basis points of the tariff and aluminum headwind in Q3 and Q4 are already locked through your price-lock agreements versus still floating with the LME?"

- **Rationale:** Management cited "price locks across the back half" and FIFO benefit in Q3 but never sized the locked-versus-floating split, which is essential for modeling the H2 margin path.
- **Question Type:** Presumptive Question with Component Breakdown
- **Question Topic(s):** Gross margin, tariffs, aluminum, price locks
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
- **Question Importance Rank:** 1

**Topic: Celsius Brand Growth Quality**

**Q (Good):** "Reported Celsius brand revenue grew 9% against ~3% scanner growth on a favorable distributor comp — in which specific quarter does the reported figure converge back to the scanner rate?"

- **Rationale:** The CFO disclosed the reported-versus-scanner gap but did not say when the favorable distributor comp fully washes out, which is needed to forecast the Celsius brand standalone trajectory.
- **Question Type:** Context Reinstatement + Time-Bounding
- **Question Topic(s):** Celsius brand growth, distributor inventory, scanner data
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
- **Question Importance Rank:** 2

**Topic: Alani Nu Back-Half Pacing**

**Q (Good):** "Against the 2024 Alani Nu quarterly progression of $146M / $166M / $157M, what core-portfolio growth rate (excluding LTOs) are you assuming for Q3 and Q4?"

- **Rationale:** The CFO said core-portfolio growth is "solid" but never separated LTO-driven from core growth, leaving the durability of Alani Nu's back-half base unquantified.
- **Question Type:** Baseline Establishment + Component Breakdown
- **Question Topic(s):** Alani Nu revenue, LTO contribution, core velocity
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 2
- **Question Importance Rank:** 3

**Topic: Earn-Out Re-measurement**

**Q (Good):** "With the contingent consideration already re-measured to the $25 million maximum, what additional 2025 net-sales upside, if any, is no longer captured by the earn-out cap?"

- **Rationale:** Management explained the earn-out methodology but did not clarify whether further Alani Nu outperformance creates any incremental P&L charge or is fully capped.
- **Question Type:** Scenario Testing
- **Question Topic(s):** Contingent consideration, G&A, Alani Nu net sales
- **Topic Importance Rank:** 3
- **Question Importance Rank:** 4
