## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** At its 2023 Annual General Meeting, Celsius management delivered a financial recap rather than fielding live questions (no shareholder questions were submitted). CFO Jarrod Langhans reported full-year 2022 revenue of approximately $654 million, up 108% from $314 million, with North America contributing approximately 126% of growth; gross profit rose 111% to $271 million, and the year included $194 million of distribution-network termination expenses (funded by Pepsi but expensed immediately under GAAP while the offsetting Pepsi funds are capitalized and amortized over the distribution agreement's life). For Q1 2023, revenue was approximately $260 million, up 95%, with North America revenue of $249 million up 101%; gross margin improved to approximately 44% from approximately 40% a year earlier on lower average can prices and orbit-model leverage, partly offset by higher freight and a few million dollars of inventory write-offs. CEO John Fieldly framed 2022 as a "transformational year" and laid out three shareholder-value drivers — revenue growth, operational excellence and EBITDA growth — while highlighting a three-year revenue CAGR of 105%, ACV expansion from 69.5% to 95.4%, a doubling of energy-category share to 7.5 (a strong #3 brand), and a 19.1 Amazon share. Headwinds acknowledged were modest: higher freight, inventory write-offs, increased days-inventory-outstanding at the primary distributor's mixing centers, and rising G&A from building a scalable back office. The strategic outlook is confident and growth-oriented, centered on channel expansion (club, food service, college/universities), innovation (the expanding Vibe line), and global expansion via the Pepsi partnership, Asia partners and a Nordics-based European team. The board was re-elected and Ernst & Young ratified as auditor.

- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:** No analyst or shareholder questions were asked. Toby David solicited questions via the online portal; "seeing that there have been no questions presented for our review," the meeting proceeded directly to the voting results. There is therefore no analyst-focus content for this event.

- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:** None. There was no Q&A session and no points of friction; the meeting was procedural and the financial recap was uncontested.

- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **Club channel scaling in 2023 and beyond:** Management called the newly entered club channel a source of "great opportunities and further scale for '23 and beyond," making club ramp a forward growth lever.
  - **Food service / college & university channel expansion:** The Pepsi partnership opened these channels and management sees "a great opportunity that lies ahead" in expanding usage occasions.
  - **International expansion:** Management cited a growing global opportunity via the Pepsi partnership, Asia strategic partners, and a dedicated Nordics team with a European expansion playbook — a future growth vector.
  - **Operational-excellence margin initiatives:** Direct co-packer-to-distributor shipping, vertical integration and scrap-waste efficiency are positioned to drive gross profit improvement.

- **Year-over-Year Comparability Factors:**
  - **Tough Comparisons:** Not explicitly framed, though the 108% FY2022 and 95% Q1 2023 revenue growth rates set a high bar for future YoY comparisons.
  - **Easy Comparisons:** Not explicitly framed.
  - **Timing Shifts:** Q1 2023 sales and marketing expense was offset by lower sales-related expense "due to timing of our activation"; marketing investment timing shifts between quarters.
  - **Distribution & Channel Dynamics:** ACV expanded from 69.5% to 95.4% year over year; convenience-channel ACV gained 37.7 points to 93.4% from 55.7%; the Pepsi integration opened new C&G and food-service channels. Days-inventory-outstanding rose at the primary distributor's mixing centers relative to year-end 2022.

- **Structural & Reporting Comparability Factors:**
  - **Currency & Pricing Impacts:** FY2022 gross margin benefited from "some pricing benefits"; gross-margin improvement also came from lower average can costs as higher-priced international cans worked out of inventory.
  - **Portfolio & Mix Changes:** Celsius entered the club channel in 2022; the Pepsi partnership opened food-service and college/university channels.
  - **Accounting & Calendar Effects:** The most material reporting item — the $194 million of distribution-network termination expenses in FY2022 sales and marketing — must be expensed immediately under GAAP, while the offsetting Pepsi funding is capitalized and amortized over the life of the distribution agreement, creating a significant timing mismatch. Excluding the $194 million, FY2022 S&M as a percentage of sales was consistent year over year.
  - **Macro & Seasonal Anomalies:** Management cited health-and-wellness and better-for-you consumer trends as structural tailwinds; no specific seasonal anomalies were quantified.
