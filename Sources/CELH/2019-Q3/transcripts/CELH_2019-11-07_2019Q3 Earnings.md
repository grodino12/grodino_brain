## STEP 4: Executive Summary

- **Management's Core Message:** Management presented Q3 2019 as a record quarter, with revenue of $20.4 million (up 23% reported, or 45% excluding one-time items) and the company's return to positive net income of approximately $1 million ($0.02 per basic share) versus a $4.2 million loss in Q3 2018. The primary positive driver was North American growth of 48% (66% on a normalized basis), fueled by distribution expansion across a rapidly growing DSD network (now 70+ partners, up from ~6 in February), new retail doors (over 60,000 locations, up 57% in 2019), and strong velocity in the convenience channel where SPINS data showed 41.1% growth, 4.3x the category. Headwinds acknowledged included an 11% decline in European revenue (timing of flavor launches, promotions and orders, since remediated) and lower Asian revenue from the shift to a China royalty/license model — though the China shift was margin-accretive as it removed direct marketing spend. The strategic outlook centered on the post-quarter acquisition of Nordic partner Func Food Group for ~$24.2 million, which lifts pro forma consolidated revenue run rate to ~$100 million and adds the FAST protein-bar business. Management's tone was highly optimistic and confident about momentum heading into 2020 buyer-season planogram resets.
- **Analyst Focus & Key Questions:**
  - Cash generation sustainability and whether the reported cash balance was post-acquisition (Question 1).
  - The pace and milestones of DSD network buildout, stock-out remediation, and Target/CVS conversion to DSD (Questions 2, 3, 4).
  - Func Food economics — debt carry cost, revenue run rate, and growth trajectory (Questions 5, 6).
  - FAST protein-snack portfolio timeline and North America launch channels (Question 7).
  - International sales ex-China and the competitive positioning of the HEAT performance line versus Bang (Questions 9, 10).
- **Key Areas of Debate or Tension:** The call was largely congratulatory with little adversarial tension. Mild probing points included:
  - Whether cash generation is durable given working-capital reinvestment needs (Question 1).
  - How far along the DSD buildout truly is — management quantified it as roughly "a third" of the 250–300 distributors needed (Question 5).
  - Whether the FAST/protein-snack launch had been accelerated versus prior "it would be a while" commentary; management reaffirmed a deliberate, methodical, EBITDA-protective approach (Question 7).
- **Potential Catalysts & Inflection Points:**
  - **Func Food consolidation in Q4 2019:** First quarter of consolidated Nordic revenue (from October 24); a test of integration and the ~$25M conservative annualized revenue add.
  - **2020 planogram resets / buyer season:** Management said buyers are rationalizing 2020 category sets now and Celsius is a "top pick" — successful resets would materially expand shelf space and ACV.
  - **DSD network national coverage within 12 months:** Reaching majority US coverage; management expects ~40% sales lift in outlets converted to DSD.
  - **BCAA recovery line and FAST bar launches in 2020:** New category entries management expects to "add meaningful growth in 2020."
- **Year-over-Year Comparability Factors:**
  - **Tough Comparisons:** Prior year Q3 2018 North America included ~$1.3 million of sales shifted from Q2 2018 into Q3 2018, inflating the prior-year base; Q3 2018 Asia included ~$1.35 million of sales versus only ~$0.2 million of non-China sales in 2019 due to the China royalty-model shift.
  - **Easy Comparisons:** Q3 2018 carried a $4.2 million net loss and a negative adjusted EBITDA of $2.9 million, making 2019 profitability comparisons favorable; Europe was down 11% on product-availability issues "since remediated."
  - **Timing Shifts:** Func Food acquisition closed October 24/25, 2019 — consolidated revenue begins in Q4 2019 and is NOT in Q3 results, a structural step-up for Q4.
  - **Distribution & Channel Dynamics:** DSD partners grew from ~6 (February) to 70+; AB independent wholesaler network grew from 6 to 31 since February; Kroger expanded to 1,100+ locations; 3,300 incremental convenience stores added (Circle K SE, Flying J, Pilot). Management cited ~40% expected sales lift in outlets converting to DSD.
- **Structural & Reporting Comparability Factors:**
  - **Currency & Pricing Impacts:** Revenue growth was driven primarily by volume, not pricing. A translation loss/gain on the China note receivable affected adjusted EBITDA reconciliation (~$1,900 quarterly; ~$12.1M cumulative gain on the note receivable in nine-month figures).
  - **Portfolio & Mix Changes:** China shifted to a royalty/license model in early 2019, reducing Asia revenue but also removing ~$5.1M of quarterly marketing spend (~$8.1M for nine months); Func Food acquisition (~$24.2M; ~1x revenue) adds ~$25M+ annualized revenue and the FAST protein-bar business beginning Q4 2019.
  - **Accounting & Calendar Effects:** Other income of $11.3 million in the nine-month period reflects the China note receivable recognized at the start of 2019 — a non-operating item that distorts year-to-date net income comparisons.
  - **Macro & Seasonal Anomalies:** Management noted Q4 holiday season is traditionally slower in retail with more novelty competition; large production runs were scheduled October–December ahead of historically strong Q1/Q2 demand.

#### Follow-Up Questions

**Topic: Func Food Consolidated Margin**
- **Q:** "When Func Food consolidates in Q4 2019, what gross margin does the FAST protein-bar business carry relative to your stated ~42% beverage gross margin?"
  - **Rationale:** Management said the deal lets them "maintain our historical gross profit margins," but protein bars structurally differ from beverages; modeling the blended margin requires this detail.
  - **Question Type:** Presumptive Question with Baseline Establishment
  - **Question Topic(s):** Gross margin, portfolio mix, Func Food integration
  - **Topic Importance Rank:** 1
  - **Question Importance Rank:** 1

**Topic: DSD Conversion Economics**
- **Q:** "Of the ~40% sales lift you expect in DSD-converted outlets, how much is incremental velocity versus reduced out-of-stocks?"
  - **Rationale:** Management cited a ~40% lift but did not decompose the source, which matters for sizing the recurring uplift across the remaining ~200 distributors.
  - **Question Type:** Component Breakdown
  - **Question Topic(s):** DSD network, velocity, stock-outs
  - **Topic Importance Rank:** 2
  - **Question Importance Rank:** 2

**Topic: Func Debt Servicing**
- **Q:** "On the ~$9.4 million of assumed restructured Func debt, what interest rate underlies the ~$200,000–$300,000 quarterly carry, and over what term does it amortize?"
  - **Rationale:** The CFO gave a quarterly carry range but no rate or maturity, leaving the cash-flow drag unquantified.
  - **Question Type:** Precision Technique
  - **Question Topic(s):** Capital structure, debt, Func Food
  - **Topic Importance Rank:** 3
  - **Question Importance Rank:** 3

**Topic: 2020 Planogram Reset Sizing**
- **Q:** "For the 2020 convenience planogram resets you are working on, what incremental ACV do you expect to capture versus your current 11.9% ACV?"
  - **Rationale:** Management framed resets as a major 2020 opportunity but gave no quantified ACV target, which is the key driver of convenience-channel growth.
  - **Question Type:** Baseline Establishment
  - **Question Topic(s):** ACV, convenience channel, shelf space
  - **Topic Importance Rank:** 2
